About Habit Stacker

Big changes rarely come from big leaps. They come from small, steady steps repeated over time. Habit Stacker grew out of that idea, inspired by James Clear's Atomic Habits and one strategy from it in particular: habit stacking.

The idea behind it

Habit stacking means attaching a new habit to something you already do every day. You don't have to rely on willpower or wait until you feel motivated. Your existing routines become the cues that remind you to act.

  • "After I pour my morning coffee, I'll write down one goal for the day."
  • "After I brush my teeth, I'll stretch for two minutes."
  • "After I sit down at my desk, I'll review my to-do list."

Each new habit is linked to one that's already part of your day, so it becomes easier to remember and easier to keep.

Why we built it

We're a team of students who know what it's like to juggle classes, work, and everything in between. Like a lot of people, we'd set goals with great intentions, only to watch them fade after a week or two. We realized the problem usually wasn't a lack of motivation. It was the lack of a system that fit into our real, busy lives. Habit Stacker is the tool we wished we had: simple, encouraging, and designed around the way habits actually form.

What Habit Stacker helps you do

  • Find your anchors: identify the routines you already do every day without thinking.
  • Stack new habits: link small, realistic actions to those anchors so they feel natural.
  • Track your progress: build streaks, see your consistency grow, and celebrate the small wins.
  • Start small: aim to get just 1% better each day. Progress matters more than perfection.

Our belief

As James Clear puts it, success comes down to the systems you build, not just the goals you set. A missed day doesn't erase your progress, and a tiny habit done consistently can grow into something life-changing. We hope Habit Stacker helps you build the kind of systems that make your goals feel within reach, one small habit at a time.
